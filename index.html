Meet Divya – The Heart Behind Divya Travels

Divya, the passionate owner of Divya Travels, is a seasoned globetrotter whose travel experiences span across Europe, Asia and India. With a deep love for exploring cultures, destinations and unforgettable experiences, Divya has personally visited some of the world's most iconic places.

Her international journeys include Switzerland, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, China, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Bali, Philippines, Japan, South Korea, Russia, Turkey and Egypt.

Across India, she has explored destinations including Goa, Dharamshala, Manali, Kashmir, Ladakh, Jaipur, Udaipur and Agra, gaining valuable travel knowledge that helps create memorable experiences for every traveler.

At Divya Travels, we believe travel is more than just reaching a destination — it's about creating memories, discovering cultures and experiencing the world with comfort and confidence.
